# Billing rules

Owned by the finance team. The invoicing code implements these.

## Late fees

- An invoice that is overdue is charged 2% of its amount for each **full** 30 days it
  is overdue. Fewer than 30 days overdue means no fee.
- The fee never exceeds 10% of the invoice amount.
- Fees are computed in whole cents, rounded down.

## Refunds

- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days.
