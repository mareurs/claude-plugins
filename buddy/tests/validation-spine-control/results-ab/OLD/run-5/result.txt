## One thing to confirm before you refactor

The docstring's two sentences jointly pin the window to `[due, due+5)` — "not yet due is not in grace" fixes the start at `due`, and "GRACE_DAYS days long" then forces the exit at `due+5`. That's what I tested, and it matches current behavior.

But if the business rule is actually *"five days of grace **after** the due date"*, the exit belongs at `due+6` and the current code is off by one — in which case `test_window_by_day_offset[5-False]` is locking in a bug rather than protecting a contract. I derived the expectation from the docstring rather than from the code, but the docstring is the only spec I have here. Worth a glance at the ticket or the invoicing policy before you lean on these tests.
