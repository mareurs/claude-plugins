## Properties to add beside the examples

- **Cross-format equivalence (metamorphic).** For any seeded rows, parse the CSV back with `csv.reader` and parse the JSON. Both must carry the same ids and totals, compared as strings. This is the property that surfaces the `None` divergence above.
